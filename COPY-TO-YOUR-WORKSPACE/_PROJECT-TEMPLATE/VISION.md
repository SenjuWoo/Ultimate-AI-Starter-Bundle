# Vision contract

The persistent art-direction and design source of truth for a creative project.
`STATE.md` links here; it does not repeat it. Ordinary utility or infrastructure
projects do not need this file.

## Core experience

What the player or viewer should feel, and for whom. One paragraph.

## Creative pillars

The three to five things that must stay true in every decision. Each pillar has a
test: "would this change break it?"

## Interaction and structure

Gameplay loop, or the structural/topological requirements that are not negotiable
in this project. State them plainly; do not illustrate with another game's layout.

## Visual language

Silhouettes, shapes, proportions, materials, colours, lighting, contrast, density,
UI style. Short and concrete enough to check against a screenshot.

## Audio and motion

Only where they matter. Otherwise say "not defined yet" rather than inventing one.

## References

Every reference names the exact contribution it is meant to make — palette,
silhouette, lighting, pacing — not "the vibe".

## Approved and rejected examples

- Approved: what, where the asset lives if there is one, why it was approved.
- Rejected: what, why, date. A rejected direction stays rejected until the user
  reverses it.

This table starts empty on purpose. A row here is a binding decision for ONE
project, so it belongs in that project's own VISION.md, never in this template.

This table is the anti-regression record. A later agent that reverts a listed
correction is wrong even if its own reasoning is sound; ask before changing it.

## Freedom

Where the agent may invent without asking.

## Quality targets

What "good" looks like, and which artefact proves it (screenshot, playtest note,
render). Text alone cannot replace looking at the result.
